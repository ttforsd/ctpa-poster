1. Diagnostic Pathway Adherence

  • Two-Level Wells Score: Please ensure this is calculated and documented prior to requesting advanced imaging.
  • Wells > 4.0: CTPA is clinically indicated.
  • Wells ≤ 4.0: A D-Dimer assay is a requisite preliminary investigation.
  • Threshold Guidance: The clinical audit highlighted instances where CTPA was requested despite D-Dimer values remaining below the required referral threshold.
MDCalc Two-Level PE Wells Score

2. Clinical Stewardship & Safety

  • Clinical Context: 68% of local CTPA scans demonstrated alternative pathology. Such findings offer supplementary diagnostic utility rather than serving as the primary justification for a CTPA referral.
  • Radiation Considerations: CTPA delivers a substantially higher dose of ionising radiation compared to baseline modalities like a chest XR (roughly 40-80x higher), and exposes the patient to similar radiation levels as a standard chest CT.
  • Patient Safety: Please consider alternative, lower-risk imaging modalities if the clinical suspicion for pulmonary embolism is low.
